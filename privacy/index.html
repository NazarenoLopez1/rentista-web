Rentista Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · Versión en castellano

This Policy describes how data is handled for users of the Rentista application (the "App"), developed by Xicarron Studio Games (the "Developer"). The App is not directed to children under 13.

1. Data collected by the App

The App does not collect, store or transmit personal data to the Developer. It requires no registration or user account and includes no analytics tools.

Game progress, high scores and preferences are stored solely on the device and are deleted when the App is uninstalled.

2. Advertising

The App displays advertising provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC ("Google"). To serve ads and measure their performance, Google may process the device's advertising identifier, technical information about the device, the IP address — from which approximate location may be inferred — and ad interaction data.

This processing is carried out under Google's sole responsibility. The Developer does not access, store or share that data.

See Google's Privacy Policy and details on how its partners use this data.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the App requests prior consent through Google's official form before enabling personalized ads.

3. User choices

  • Limit personalized advertising and reset or delete the advertising identifier through the device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Delete stored data by uninstalling the App or clearing its data from the system settings.

Depending on the country of residence, applicable law may grant rights of access, rectification, erasure or objection regarding the data described in section 2. Since Google processes that data, such requests must be addressed to Google through the channels listed in its privacy policy.

4. Changes

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. The version in force is the one published at this address, with its last updated date shown at the top.
